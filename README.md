# DILLIGAD: A Chrome extension for mindful browsing

![banner](/icons/icon-128.png)

## About
DILLIGAD is a wonderfully whimsical browser extension that can randomly spawn tiny ducks on your webpage. These ducks can be set to appear randomly or based on a timer that can help remind you to drink water or get up and move around instead of sitting around all day. There are so many ducks out there to meet and collect and keep in your duck barn. I guess I really do give a duck.

## Inspiration
We enjoy watching videos of people hiding tiny ducks around houses and office spaces for their family or co-workers to find unexpectedly. So we wondered, why not make something that hides ducks in random places on the internet? The name DILLIGAD was inspired by a very old Australian comedian Kevin Wilson's song [DILLIGAF](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7a6EOyaMdqY) that was later turned into a [reel/meme](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DVVQqQWkgnp/?igsh=ejYyM3pkNDdidW5u).

## How to add DILLIGAD to your Chrome browser
### Step 1
Clone this repository or download the main branch as a .zip folder.

### Step 2.
In Google Chrome, navigate to `chrome://extensions/` and enable developer mode.
Click the "Load unpacked" button and select the DILLIGAD folder.

### Step 3.
Go to any website. Experience joy. (Please go duck yourself 😊).

## Using DILLIGAD
Once you have the extension installed, you can activate the extension by opening it.

Click on the chrome extension button on the right side of the search bar and pin DILLIGAD onto your extension bar.

When you encounter a random duck on any webpage you visit, make sure you click on it to add it to your barn!

Clicking on the extension will open up your barn view and show you the ducks you have collected so far.

Click on the available duck icons to learn their name and a little bit about them!
